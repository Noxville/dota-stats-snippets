Elo shift against games played

Elo shift is what a hero's games did to the Elo of the teams that picked it. A hero that keeps turning up on the winning side of upsets carries a positive shift; a hero drafted by teams that then lose to weaker opposition carries a negative one. Because it is scaled by the opponent, it separates heroes that good teams simply happen to pick from heroes whose games actually went well.[1]

The chart below places all 48 heroes picked at least ten times at The International 2026 on those two axes: average shift across the horizontal, games played up the vertical. The vertical line marks zero shift.

Method

Elo shift is averaged per game, so it is comparable across heroes with different sample sizes; it is not summed. Every game in this sample carries Elo data, so games played and Elo games are the same number here. The filter is the one on the datdota page: patch 7.41, tier 1 and 2 events, The International 2026, and a 10-game threshold.[1]
